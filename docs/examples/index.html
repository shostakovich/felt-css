Examples

Three small apps built from nothing but felt-css and Bootstrap's JavaScript, modelled on the projects felt-css was made for. Switch look and color mode on each page; view the source and copy what you like.

Dashboard#

A smart-home dashboard: stat tiles for solar and battery, switches for lamps, weather, a device list and today's energy table. With plush icons.

Shared expenses#

A mobile-first app for splitting costs: balances, an expense list, a sewn-on tab bar and an "add expense" form with input groups and checks.

Blog#

A personal blog: featured post, article prose with figures, quotes and code, an archive sidebar and pagination.

Bootstrap, but made of wool felt. Public domain under The Unlicense.

The structure, wording and many examples of these pages are adapted from the Bootstrap 5.3 documentation by the Bootstrap team (code MIT, docs CC BY 3.0), trimmed to what felt-css supports.

Plush icons from ZiWoAS.
