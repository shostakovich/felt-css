Basic example

Wrap a series of buttons with .btn in .btn-group.

Button groups require an appropriate role attribute and explicit label to ensure assistive technologies like screen readers identify buttons as grouped and announce them. Use role="group" for button groups or role="toolbar" for button toolbars. Then use aria-label or aria-labelledby to label them.

These classes can also be added to groups of links, as an alternative to the .nav navigation components.

In the felt look a group of filled buttons is cut from one piece of felt with a single seam around it and a pressed fold between the buttons. A group of toggles (.btn-check, outline or light buttons) becomes a groove pressed into the felt, and the chosen segment is a small patch sewn into it.

Checkbox and radio button groups

Combine button-like checkbox and radio toggle buttons into a seamless looking button group.

The toggles work with every button variant, for instance the plain .btn or .btn-light for a quiet segmented control.

Button toolbar

Combine sets of button groups into button toolbars for more complex components. Use utility classes as needed to space out groups, buttons, and more.

Feel free to mix input groups with button groups in your toolbars. Similar to the example above, you’ll likely need some utilities though to space things properly.

@

felt-css's .btn-toolbar already puts a small gap between its groups, so the margin utilities are optional.

Sizing

Instead of applying button sizing classes to every button in a group, just add .btn-group-lg or .btn-group-sm to each .btn-group, including each one when nesting multiple groups.

Nesting

Place a .btn-group within another .btn-group when you want dropdown menus mixed with a series of buttons.
