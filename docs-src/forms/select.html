Default

Custom <select> menus need only a custom class, .form-select to trigger the custom styles. Custom styles are limited to the <select>'s initial appearance and cannot modify the <option>s due to browser limitations.

The chevron is an image, so felt-css ships one per look and theme (slate grey in clean, navy or cream in felt) and swaps it with the --felt-select-chevron custom property. In the felt look the select also gets the field's felt texture.

Sizing

You may also choose from small and large custom selects to match the similarly sized text inputs.
