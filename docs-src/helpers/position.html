Fixed bottom

Position an element at the bottom of the viewport, from edge to edge. Be sure you understand the ramifications of fixed position in your project; you may need to add additional CSS.

...

For a bar at the bottom of a phone screen, add .pb-safe: it pads the bar by at least .5rem, and more where the device needs room for its home indicator (env(safe-area-inset-bottom)). Your page needs viewport-fit=cover in its viewport meta tag for the inset to be reported.

...

A .navbar with .fixed-top or .fixed-bottom loses its rounded corners and side borders, so in felt it reads as a strip of felt sewn along its inner edge.
